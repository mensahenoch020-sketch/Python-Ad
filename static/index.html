👋 How to use this app

  1. Set up once. Tap Today → Set up and enter your niche, how many videos a week you can post, your audience, and timezone.
  2. Install on your phone. In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It then opens full-screen like a real app.
  3. Each morning open Today. It shows your next posting slot and a countdown.
  4. Plan a video. Go to Ideas, type a topic, and pick a high-scoring hook. Build a caption with the Caption builder.
  5. Before you post, open Plan → run the checklist, grab a hashtag set, and score your idea (aim for 8+/10).
  6. Post at your scheduled slot. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar in Plan so your slots show up with reminders.
  7. After 24-48h, log it. Go to Log and enter views/likes/comments. After 10 logged posts the schedule & stats personalise to YOUR audience.
  8. Weekly, check Stats — double down on your best day, format, and hashtags.

The 3 non-negotiables

  • Hook: earn the first 2 seconds or nothing else matters.
  • Completion: short + a payoff = shown to more people.
  • Consistency: 1 post/day for 30 days beats 30 in one day.

No app can guarantee views — TikTok's ranking is private. This is a coach + analytics tool, with no bots or fake engagement. Good content + consistency does the heavy lifting.
